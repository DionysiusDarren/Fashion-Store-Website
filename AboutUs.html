Let's Know Each Other!

Who Are We?


Christian Wijaya is a luxury fashion house that stands at the crossroads of timeless elegance and contemporary innovation that is founded in 2012. Headquartered in Jakarta, Indonesia, the brand has rapidly gained global recognition for its masterful blend of classic tailoring, avant-garde design, and uncompromising quality. At Christian Wijaya, every garment tells a story—of precision, passion, and perfection. From bespoke evening wear to iconic leather goods and statement footwear, each piece is crafted to embody quiet confidence and refined sophistication. Our collections are not just worn—they are experienced.


Our Philosophy


Luxury With Purpose

Christian Wijaya believes that luxury should never come at the cost of responsibility. As part of our ongoing commitment to sustainable fashion, we prioritize eco-conscious materials, ethical labor practices, and low-impact production techniques. From locally sourced organic fabrics to upcycled leather and recyclable packaging, sustainability is woven into the very fabric of our brand.

Artistry Meets Innovation

We collaborate with artisans and innovators alike, integrating centuries-old craftsmanship with cutting-edge textile technology. This fusion results in collections that are as rich in story as they are in style.
